Unlock once, then reveal freely. Touch ID or your password now unlocks the inventory, the way a password manager does. While it's unlocked, Reveal shows a value, its excerpt and its source without asking again, and Hide or choosing another value only hides them again. Unlock and Lock sit at the bottom of the sidebar. The app still locks after 5 minutes idle, when the window closes, or when the Mac sleeps or locks.
