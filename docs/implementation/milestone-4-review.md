Review findings resolved by the implementation owners:

- Capture admission now obtains its processing permit before reading the current live scope. Resume installs its scope before rotating the store generation, so a pre-resume permit cannot commit with a later scope.
- Coverage persistence keeps the full gap, including its capability and interval, rather than only its reason. Observed history bounds and unread status are shown separately from a claim of continuous coverage.
- Codex controlled errors map to source/version/correlation/budget/malformed coverage states. A validated hook selection joins monitoring even when canonical public content has not flushed yet.
- Claude clamps cached and newly read progress bounds to the current audit window. The unchanged-source regression prevents an earlier rolling audit from publishing dates outside a later one.

The capture completion transaction replaces the queued capture, advances supplied checkpoints, and accumulates protected history progress together after source commits. Queue replacement does not allow a checkpoint to lead durable source processing, and a stale permit rejects prepared continuations.
