Review asks one question with two outcomes. "Does this need rotating?" Needs rotation moves the value to To rotate. Every other answer moves it to Done, where later appearances stay quiet, and the bar now says so. The other answers are reasons: Not a secret, Test or dev only, and, from the menu, No longer works (expired, revoked or rotated) and Real, kept as is. No longer works also has its own key, X. To rotate has one button, Mark rotated or revoked (D), instead of two. Rotated and revoked values show as "No longer works" in Done, Accepted risk shows as "Real, kept as is", and values in Done go Back to review instead of changing their decision.
