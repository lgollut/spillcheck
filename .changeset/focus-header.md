Keep the Focus header current and unobstructed. Its title now counts the values still left, such as "105 values to review", and changes with each decision and undo; before, it kept the count from when the session started. On macOS 26, scrolling a long excerpt in Focus, or a long Coverage or Settings page, no longer draws the content over the header at the top of the window, where it covered the previous and next buttons and the progress bar.
