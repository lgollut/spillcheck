Review values in an inbox. The list is split into To review, To rotate and Done, each with its count. Values that appeared since you last looked have an unread dot and come first, which replaces the "While you were away" box. The detail leads with the evidence: what was found and why, the value inside the context where it appeared, then every conversation that printed it. Decide with the buttons or the T, N and R keys; the next value then opens, and Undo, or Command-Z, takes the decision back. A value to rotate offers Mark rotated, D, and Mark revoked once you've changed it at its service. Opening and Resume in Terminal now sit in each conversation's menu. Entries are numbered #12 instead of L-12, and matches are described as Known format, Unsure match or Generic pattern.
