Go through values one at a time in Focus. Start it with Focus or F from To review or To rotate, or with "Go through one by one" after selecting several values. Each value shows with its evidence; T, N and R decide, D marks a value rotated, the arrows move back and forth, and Escape returns to the inbox. A session ends with a summary of what you decided and, after a review, offers to continue with the values to rotate. You can also Command- or Shift-click values to select several and decide them together, in one step that Undo takes back.
